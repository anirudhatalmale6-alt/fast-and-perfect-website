1. Google Business Profile

The address decision. Please get this right, it is the one that can cost you the listing.

Google requires that the address on a profile is somewhere you or your staff are physically present during your posted opening hours. If Suite 2020 is a registered or virtual office, listing it as a normal business address is one of the most common reasons Google suspends a listing. It often happens months later, right when the listing has started bringing in work.

Do this instead. When Google asks "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?", answer No. You are what Google calls a service area business. You still enter the address so Google can verify you exist, then it stays hidden, and customers see your service areas instead.

This also keeps your original requirement intact. Nothing of yours appears on a map.

5. Google Ads

My honest advice: do not start yet. Three reasons.

You have no reviews. Someone clicking a paid ad sees a business with no reviews and usually goes back and picks one with forty. You would be paying for clicks that convert badly.

Your Google Business Profile is not live. Once it is, you get free Maps traffic, and ads perform better alongside a real listing.

Your licence and insurance are still in progress. Worth having those settled before you spend money sending strangers to your phone.

Start ads once you have the profile live and five or so reviews. The same money will go considerably further. Everything below is ready for when that day comes.

Campaign structure

TypeSearch only. No Display, Google will try to opt you in, turn it off.
LocationEdmonton plus 20 km. Set targeting to "Presence: people in your targeted locations", not the default.
BudgetStart at 20 to 30 dollars a day. Ignore Google's suggestion, it is always higher.
Ad groupsOne per service. Do not mix them, it wrecks relevance.

Keywords

House cleaning: "house cleaning edmonton", "cleaning services edmonton", "home cleaners edmonton", "maid service edmonton", "deep cleaning edmonton"

Move out: "move out cleaning edmonton", "move in cleaning edmonton", "end of lease cleaning edmonton"

Carpet: "carpet cleaning edmonton", "carpet cleaner edmonton", "upholstery cleaning edmonton"

Commercial: "office cleaning edmonton", "commercial cleaning edmonton", "janitorial services edmonton"

Use phrase match, in quotes, not broad match. Broad match is how new advertisers lose money fastest.

Negative keywords, add these on day one

free, jobs, job, hiring, salary, career, careers, training, course, courses, how to, diy, equipment, supplies, for sale, rental, rent, franchise, business for sale, cheap, student, part time, resume

Without these you pay for people looking for cleaning jobs, not cleaning services. It is the most common way a new account burns its budget.
