Fast and Perfect Ltd. is a locally operated cleaning company serving Edmonton and surrounding areas. Our goal is to provide reliable, detailed and professional cleaning services with clear pricing and an easy booking experience.

We serve residential and commercial clients and focus on delivering consistent, high-quality service every time.

Carpet and upholstery work is done with a professional portable hot-water extractor rather than a domestic machine — that is what lifts soil out of the pile instead of moving it around. A portable unit also reaches apartments, upper floors and units a truck-mounted hose cannot.

Every job starts with a written quote and an agreed task list, so there is no ambiguity about what is included or what it costs.
